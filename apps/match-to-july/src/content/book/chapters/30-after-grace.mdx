Direct Loans enter repayment when the six-month [grace period](/read/glossary/grace-period) after graduation ends ([34 CFR 685.207](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-685.207)). For a May 2027 graduate that is November 2027, four months into residency. The rules below are as in effect on 2026-10-21, under Public Law 119-21 (signed 2025-07-04) and the RISE final rule (published 2026-05-01, effective 2026-07-01) ([Pub. L. 119-21](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/PLAW-119publ21/html/PLAW-119publ21.htm); [91 FR 23768](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/05/01/2026-08556/reimagining-and-improving-student-education-federal-student-loan-program-final-regulations)).

## Which plans are open

- **[RAP](/read/glossary/rap)**, available since 2026-07-01, to any Direct Loan borrower except for parent PLUS loans ([20 U.S.C. 1087e(q)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/20/1087e); [34 CFR 685.209](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-685.209)).
- **[Tiered Standard](/read/glossary/tiered-standard)**, a fixed schedule set by balance ([34 CFR 685.208](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-685.208)). ED's position is that it does not qualify for [PSLF](/read/glossary/pslf).
- **IBR**, only for loans made before 2026-07-01 and only while the borrower has no Direct Loan first disbursed on or after that date in repayment ([20 U.S.C. 1098e](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/20/1098e); [34 CFR 685.209](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-685.209)). A 2027 graduate who borrowed for the 2026–27 year falls outside it.
- **Mandatory [residency forbearance](/read/glossary/residency-forbearance)**, up to 12 months at a time for the length of training ([34 CFR 685.205(a)(3)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-685.205)).

Consolidating on or after 2026-07-01 produces a loan repayable only under RAP or Tiered Standard.

## Side by side

For a single May 2027 graduate on a $70,000 stipend, with no dependents and a student-year AGI of $10,000 or less, the [Match money plan](https://www.joinandwise.com/residency/plan/texas) gives:

| Item | RAP | Residency forbearance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly payment, Nov 2027 – Oct 2028 | $10 (the minimum) | $0 |
| After first recertification (2027 return) | about $76 | $0 |
| After second recertification (2028 return) | about $344 | $0 |
| Interest not covered by the payment | Not charged for on-time, full payments | Accrues: about $672.50 a month per $100,000 at 8.07% |
| PSLF months, at a qualifying employer | Counted | Not counted |
| Earliest PSLF date, if every month from grace qualifies | November 2037 | Three years of forbearance moves it to November 2040 |

The waiver and the $50 principal match are in [20 U.S.C. 1087e(q)(2)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/20/1087e) and [34 CFR 685.209](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-685.209). PSLF's 120 payments and its employer test are in [20 U.S.C. 1087e(m)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/20/1087e) and [34 CFR 685.219](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-685.219). The [repayment comparison](https://www.joinandwise.com/loans/repayment) runs the same rules on the reader's own balance.

## What is unsettled

As of 2026-10-21:

- Whether a borrower in the grace period can enter RAP or IBR early without consolidating is unconfirmed.
- The 2025 PSLF employer rule was vacated on 2026-06-30 and is on appeal ([CRS LSB11472](https://www.everycrsreport.com/reports/LSB11472.html)).
- ED has said it will clarify how overpayments interact with RAP's interest waiver and match.

## Questions for the servicer
