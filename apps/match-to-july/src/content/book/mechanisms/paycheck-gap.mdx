## Scope

The gap is arithmetic on three dates: the end of the graduation month, the start date in the residency agreement, and the first payday. Andwise's [Match money plan](https://www.joinandwise.com/residency/plan/texas) models a May graduation and a first paycheck three weeks after a July 1 start: May 31 to July 22 is **52 days**. A program that pays two weeks after the start shortens that to about 45; one that pays at the end of July lengthens it to about 60. The payroll calendar comes from the program's GME office.

The same plan's default inputs, which are assumptions to replace, total $10,589: $2,500 for the move, $3,200 for a deposit and rent paid up front, $100 in license fees, and $4,789 of living costs for 52 days at $2,800 a month.

Some residency agreements include relocation pay or a living allowance; a resident who reviewed several agreements in 2023 found these clauses in some and not others ([Andwise, 2023-02-08](https://www.joinandwise.com/blog/a-medical-students-first-contract-what-to-know-about-the-gme-residency-agreement)).

## Boundaries

- The gap ends with the first paycheck. Withholding on that paycheck is [annualized withholding](/read/mechanisms/annualized-withholding).
- License fees count here as cash; the license's timing is [license lead time](/read/mechanisms/license-lead-time).
