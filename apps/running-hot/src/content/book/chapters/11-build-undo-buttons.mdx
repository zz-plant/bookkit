## Binding rule

Require rollback, pause, and restore pathways as first-class features.

## Enforceable standard

- Every high-impact action must declare an explicit rollback owner.
- Provisional restoration is available while disputes are active.
- Rollback path must be executable without manager exception requests.
- Undo usage must be logged and reviewed for pattern detection.

## Minimum metrics

- Time-to-harm vs time-to-restore ratio.
- Share of disputed actions receiving provisional rollback.
- Percentage of irreversible states created without compensating safeguards.
