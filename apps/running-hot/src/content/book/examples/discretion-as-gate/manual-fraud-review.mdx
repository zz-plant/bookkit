title: "Manual fraud review with opaque criteria"
status: draft
---

A courier account is deactivated after an automated fraud flag. Support states only that "unusual activity was detected" and routes the worker to manual review. Similar appeals produce opposite outcomes with no visible difference in submitted evidence.

Some workers are restored within a day; others wait weeks and are permanently removed without specific explanation.

- **Gate condition:** access depends on opaque reviewer judgment.
- **Variance source:** outcome changes by reviewer and channel, not just facts.
- **Recourse burden:** workers must craft persuasive narratives without knowing the decision standard.
- **Cooling redesign:** publish contestable reason codes, calibrate reviewers, and provide provisional earning restoration during review.
