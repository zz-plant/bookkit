mechanism: bureaucracy-moral-technology
name: "Paper trail leverage"
trigger: "Procedure is being used to deflect moral responsibility."
timeboxMinutes: 10
steps:
  - "Copy the policy or procedure being cited into a note."
  - "Add one sentence describing the concrete harm it permits."
  - "Ask for the named owner of the policy or the exception process."
  - "Request a written response on whether harm changes the decision."
failureLooksLike:
  - "The response is only 'we followed policy' with no named owner or exception path."
legitimateDemand:
  - "Provide the policy owner or the exception path with a timeline."
---

The goal is to make the policy owner visible, not to win a debate.
