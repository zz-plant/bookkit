mechanism: delay-as-force
domain: "Public benefits"
title: "Recertification loop as silent denial"
status: draft
---

A claimant submits recertification documents on time. The portal marks the case "pending review" for six weeks, then requests one additional item already uploaded. During the wait, benefits pause and late fees accrue on utilities.

No explicit denial is issued. Metrics report low denial rates, but many claimants abandon appeals because they cannot survive another review cycle.

- **Time used as pressure:** no decision, but continued waiting changes what people can demand.
- **Transferred cost:** families absorb bridge debt and service instability.
- **Visibility gap:** dashboard success measures decision counts, not elapsed harm.
- **Cooling redesign:** mandatory decision clock, interim continuation of core benefits, and automatic credit for agency-caused delay.
