title: "Rental assistance and duplicate proof demands"
status: draft
---

A tenant applying for rental assistance submits lease, income, and arrears notices through the city portal. Two weeks later, a partner nonprofit requests the same packet by email. After submission, a third verifier asks for identical records through a separate upload form.

Each duplicate request resets review priority. The tenant misses work to gather notarized copies already provided twice.

- **Access price:** repeated proof acts as an administrative toll.
- **Who wins:** applicants with spare time, storage discipline, and employer flexibility.
- **Who loses:** shift workers and people in unstable housing.
- **Cooling redesign:** one canonical case packet, shared case ID across agencies, and prohibition on duplicate requests without specific deficiency reason.
