mechanism: obligation-without-custody
domain: "Education"
title: "Attendance escalation without transport authority"
status: draft
---

A district flags a family for chronic absenteeism and warns of legal consequences. The family is told they are responsible for daily attendance, but the bus route has been repeatedly canceled and school transfer approvals are centralized at the district office.

Frontline staff pressure the parent to "do whatever it takes" while lacking authority to change transportation assignment, waive transfer timing rules, or authorize temporary remote participation.

- **Obligation assigned:** parent must guarantee attendance outcomes.
- **Custody withheld:** transport routing and transfer approvals remain inaccessible.
- **System effect:** legal risk is pushed onto the family while operational bottlenecks stay untouched.
- **Cooling redesign:** publish lever ownership, grant rapid temporary placement authority, and pause punitive escalation when district-controlled levers fail.
