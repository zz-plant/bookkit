## Field checklist

- Can the harmed person access recourse in under 10 minutes?
- Is there a named repair owner with authority over the failed lever?
- Does a provisional rollback exist while review is pending?
- Are duplicate proof demands capped and justified?
- Are queue resets treated as system failures, not user failures?

## Design standard

A system is safe only when volatility is dissipated by designed mechanisms rather than by human endurance.

That requires:

- funded buffers,
- inspectable decision criteria,
- bounded repair clocks,
- reversible high-impact actions,
- continuity guarantees across handoffs.

## Final claim

Resilience without internalization is extraction.

Dissipate heat with mechanisms, not martyrs.
