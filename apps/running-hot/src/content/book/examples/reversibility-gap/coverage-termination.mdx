domain: "Healthcare"
title: "Coverage termination faster than restoration"
status: draft
---

An insurer auto-terminates coverage after a data mismatch. Pharmacies reject prescriptions immediately. The member files correction documents the same day, but reinstatement takes 21 days.

During the gap, the member pays out of pocket for essential medication and skips follow-up care.

- **Asymmetry:** harmful state change is instant; repair path is slow.
- **Downstream harm:** debt, clinical instability, and avoidable emergency risk.
- **Institutional blind spot:** process treats restoration lag as administrative inconvenience.
- **Cooling redesign:** immediate provisional reinstatement upon verified dispute, plus mandatory time-to-repair threshold.
