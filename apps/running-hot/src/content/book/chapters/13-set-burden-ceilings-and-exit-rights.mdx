## Binding rule

Cap procedural burden and make exit rights practicable under stress.

## Enforceable standard

- Limit repeat documentation requests to one unless deficiency is explicit.
- Preserve queue position and submitted evidence across handoffs.
- Permit revocable consent and service exit without retaliatory lockout.
- Internalize failure costs through credits, penalties, or make-whole rules.

## Minimum metrics

- Duplicate request count per resolved case.
- Queue restart rate and associated drop-off.
- Successful exits completed within policy timeline.
