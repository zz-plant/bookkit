mechanism: delay-as-force
name: "Deadline countermove"
trigger: "A decision is stalled until your options shrink."
timeboxMinutes: 10
steps:
  - "Name the decision you need and the date it must be made."
  - "Send a written request with that date and the consequence of no response."
  - "Prepare one alternative path you can take if the date passes."
  - "If no response arrives, take the alternative without further negotiation."
failureLooksLike:
  - "You continue waiting without setting a deadline or alternative."
legitimateDemand:
  - "Respond by the stated date or accept that you will proceed with the alternative."
---

The point is to reintroduce time control in a low-drama way.
