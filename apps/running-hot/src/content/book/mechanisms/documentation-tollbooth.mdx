title: "Documentation tollbooth"
definition: "A repeated-proof mechanism in which access depends on producing and re-producing documents rather than on stable eligibility."
is:
  - "The same forms, records, or narratives are requested across multiple checkpoints without shared state."
  - "Administrative repetition acts as the price of service."
isNot:
  - "One-time verification with durable record linkage and transparent correction pathways."
  - "Targeted evidence requests tied to clearly stated legal requirements."
predictions:
  - "Resolution quality correlates with organizational literacy and document archive capacity."
  - "Frontline staff spend growing time revalidating known facts."
  - "Error rates rise when people must translate institutional jargon repeatedly."
tests:
  - "Count duplicate requests per case; above one duplicate, require explicit justification."
  - "Audit for 'already provided' events; frequent recurrences indicate tollbooth dynamics."
---

When documentation becomes a toll, access belongs to the best record-keeper rather than to the person with the legitimate claim.
