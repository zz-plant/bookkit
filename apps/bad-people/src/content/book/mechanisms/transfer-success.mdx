---
slug: transfer-success
title: "Transfer Success"
definition: "A system preserves power by moving cost, risk, or blame onto weaker parties while keeping decision authority elsewhere."
is:
  - "A recurring pattern where accountability travels downward but authority stays upward."
  - "A designed outcome that looks like failure to the harmed party but success to the system."
isNot:
  - "A one-off mistake by a single person acting alone."
  - "A situation where authority and accountability stay with the same decision-maker."
predictions:
  - "After harm, documentation tasks are assigned to the harmed party, not the decision owner."
  - "Requests for reversal are redirected to people without authority."
  - "Decision-makers stay insulated from the consequences they create."
tests:
  - "List who decided, who benefited, and who pays; if they differ, transfer success is present."
  - "Ask who can reverse the outcome within 10 minutes; if no one nearby can, transfer success is operating."
---

Mechanism notes:
- Focus on routing accountability back to decision authority.
- Examples should be swappable without changing the mechanism.
