title: "Delay as Force"
definition: "Time is used as pressure: delays exhaust options, narrow choices, and coerce agreement."
is:
  - "A pattern where waiting increases the cost of refusal."
  - "A tactic that relies on fatigue, deadlines, or uncertainty to force consent."
isNot:
  - "A legitimate wait for missing information."
  - "A neutral scheduling delay with no leverage attached."
predictions:
  - "Decisions are deferred until the harmed party has fewer alternatives."
  - "Deadlines appear suddenly after long stalls."
  - "People accept worse terms to stop the waiting."
tests:
  - "Ask what would change if a firm deadline were set early; if power shifts, delay is the lever."
  - "Ask whether the delay has a repair clock (owner + deadline + remedy); if not, treat it as transfer."
  - "Compare outcomes for those who can wait versus those who cannot; differences indicate the mechanism."
---

Mechanism notes:
- Focus on how time is weaponized, not on time itself.
