## Scope

RAP took effect on 2026-07-01 under Public Law 119-21, which added section 455(q) to the Higher Education Act ([20 U.S.C. 1087e](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/20/1087e); [Pub. L. 119-21](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/PLAW-119publ21/html/PLAW-119publ21.htm)). The rules sit in [34 CFR 685.209](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-685.209), restated by the [RISE final rule](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/05/01/2026-08556/reimagining-and-improving-student-education-federal-student-loan-program-final-regulations) of 2026-05-01.

- **Income measure:** AGI for the most recent taxable year. If AGI is unavailable or does not reasonably reflect current income, the borrower supplies other documentation (455(q)(3); 685.209(l)(6)).
- **Payment:** a percentage of the whole AGI, from 1% above $10,000 to 10% above $100,000; at $10,000 or less the annual base payment is $120. The monthly payment is that amount divided by 12, less $50 per dependent, and never below **$10**.
- **Interest:** for each on-time, full payment that does not cover the month's interest, the unpaid interest is not charged (455(q)(2)(A)). If the payment reduces principal by less than $50, ED reduces principal by up to $50 more.

The sequence, from Andwise's [loan-date model](https://www.joinandwise.com/residency) for a May 2027 graduate: the six-month grace period ends in November 2027, and the most recent return filed by then is for 2026, a student year. Payments of $10 a month run to the first recertification in November 2028, which prices them on the 2027 return (about 5.3 months of resident pay), then on a full year in November 2029. On a $70,000 stipend with $1,200 of pre-tax benefits, the [Match money plan](https://www.joinandwise.com/residency/plan/texas) estimates about $76 a month after the first recertification and $344 after the second.

IBR also uses the most recent AGI, but it is closed to a borrower who has any Direct Loan first disbursed on or after 2026-07-01, which includes a 2027 graduate who borrowed for the 2026–27 school year ([34 CFR 685.209](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-685.209); [studentaid.gov](https://studentaid.gov/announcements-events/big-updates)). For that class, the comparison is RAP, Tiered Standard or [residency forbearance](/read/mechanisms/uncounted-forbearance).

## Boundaries

- The mechanism is the one-year lag between income and its tax return. A servicer error in applying it is a separate problem.
- Whether a borrower can enter RAP before the grace period ends, without consolidating, was unconfirmed as of 2026-10-21. Consolidating on or after 2026-07-01 produces a loan repayable only under RAP or Tiered Standard.
