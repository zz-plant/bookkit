## Six mechanisms that route heat into people

1. **Obligation without custody** — responsibility without leverage.
2. **Delay as force** — elapsed time used as coercion.
3. **Documentation tollbooth** — repeated proof as access price.
4. **Discretion as gate** — opaque judgment without inspectable criteria.
5. **Reversibility gap** — fast harm, slow restoration.
6. **Attrition by queue** — structural drop-off through resets and handoffs.

Together these mechanisms explain how ordinary operations can produce durable harm without explicit malicious intent.

## Transfer channels

- **Time:** waiting, rework cycles, deadline uncertainty.
- **Administrative burden:** forms, uploads, duplicate narratives.
- **Emotional labor:** vigilance, escalation diplomacy, self-regulation.
- **Financial cost:** bridge spending, lost shifts, penalty fees.
- **Cognitive load:** personal case management and policy interpretation.
- **Reputational proof:** repeated demonstrations of legitimacy.

## Evidence protocol

For each suspected mechanism, collect four artifacts:

- one timeline with handoff points,
- one owner map for decision levers,
- one tally of repeat demands,
- one harm log covering costs incurred during delay.

Mechanisms become actionable when evidence links institutional design choices to transferred costs.
