## Why rollback comes before go-live

The rollback decision cannot be made safely under load. If the candidate is not named before the deploy starts, the team will debate fix-forward while the outage grows.

Use **identify rollback candidate** before every deploy. The candidate must be executable, not conceptual.

## The rule

- No executable rollback candidate, no go-live.
- When the deploy fails, execute the candidate, confirm metrics recover, then investigate.

## Verification

The drill should let any deploy owner state the candidate and its verification command from memory in under a minute.
