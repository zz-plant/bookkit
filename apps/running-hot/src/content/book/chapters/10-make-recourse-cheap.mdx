## Binding rule

Contestability must be a primary path, not a heroic exception.

## Enforceable standard

- One-click access to appeal entry points from every adverse decision.
- Required reason codes before appeal submission.
- No duplicate narrative requirement across appeal stages.
- No loss of core access solely for filing an appeal.

## Minimum metrics

- Median minutes to file an appeal.
- Completion rate from appeal start to decision.
- Percent of appeals requiring duplicate documentation.
