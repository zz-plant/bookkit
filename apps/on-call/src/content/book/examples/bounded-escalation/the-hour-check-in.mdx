A sev-1 about stuck queue processing keeps getting "still working on it" updates from the same two people. Nobody owns it by name, so the window never fires. Bounded escalation would name the head of platform as the target, fire the ramp at the window, and record the transfer — turning the quiet hour into a handoff with a timestamp.
