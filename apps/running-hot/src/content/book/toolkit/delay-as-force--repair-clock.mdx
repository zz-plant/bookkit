name: "Repair clock"
trigger: "When a claim is 'under review' and elapsed time is causing new damage."
timeboxMinutes: 10
steps:
  - "Record start date, current stage, and next named owner."
  - "Estimate harm per week of waiting (money, health risk, lost access, instability)."
  - "Set a visible deadline for the next binding decision, not just another update."
  - "Request interim protection that preserves baseline while review continues."
failureLooksLike:
  - "Only status updates are promised; no decision clock is set."
  - "Interim protections are denied without reason code."
legitimateDemand:
  - "A time-bounded decision path with interim safeguards and escalation contact."
---

If time is part of the harm, time must be governed like any other risk variable.
