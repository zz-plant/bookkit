The state pages at [joinandwise.com/residency](https://www.joinandwise.com/residency) are checked against each board (last on 2026-10-21) and link the board's own page. A few published rules, for scale: Texas, 60 to 120 days before the start ([22 TAC 161.55](https://www.tmb.texas.gov/apply-renew/physician/physician-apply/physician-in-training-permit-application)); DC, 90 days ([DC Health](https://dchealth.dc.gov/service/board-medicine-medical-training-license-and-registrant-application)); Florida and Illinois, 60 days ([Florida Board of Medicine](https://flboardofmedicine.gov/resident-physicians-interns-fellows-and-house-physicians/); [68 Ill. Adm. Code 1285](https://ilga.gov/ftp/JCAR/AdminCode/068/068012850A00900R.html)).

The [Match money plan](https://www.joinandwise.com/residency/plan/texas) counts the license fee in the cash needed before the first paycheck.
