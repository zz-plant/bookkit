This guide covers the non-clinical steps between Match Day and a new resident's first federal loan payment. Every rule it quotes links to its source: a statute, a regulation, a board rule, an IRS publication, or the Andwise data file that records the check date. Figures are as of 2026-10-21 unless a line says otherwise. Nothing here is a recommendation; each section ends in the numbers and the questions to put to the GME office, payroll or the loan servicer.

## Steps

1. **Match Day.** For the 2027 Main Residency Match, results arrive on March 19, 2027 ([NRMP applicant calendar](https://www.nrmp.org/match-calendars/main-residency-applicants/)). The program then sends its [residency agreement](/read/glossary/residency-agreement) (the GME agreement) and onboarding steps ([what a residency agreement covers](https://www.joinandwise.com/blog/a-medical-students-first-contract-what-to-know-about-the-gme-residency-agreement)).
2. **The license, March to April.** A [training license](/read/glossary/training-license) is required in 42 of 51 jurisdictions, may be required in 5 and is not required in 4. Of the 51, 20 publish a lead time or processing time and 27 that require or may require a license publish none; the range runs from 1 to 2 weeks of processing ([Maine](https://www.maine.gov/md/licensure/license-types)) to an application due 90 days before training ([DC](https://dchealth.dc.gov/service/board-medicine-medical-training-license-and-registrant-application)). See [license lead time](/read/mechanisms/license-lead-time).
3. **Credentialing and onboarding, April to June.** Hospital credentialing, health screening, payroll and benefits forms, and a DEA registration if the program requires one. Credentialing files commonly ask for an NPI ([NPPES](https://nppes.cms.hhs.gov/)), identification, immunization records and life-support certifications ([the physician's guide to credentialing](https://www.joinandwise.com/blog/the-physicians-guide-to-credentialing)).
4. **The move and the start.** The start date is the one in the residency agreement; orientation often begins in the last week of June. With a May graduation and a first paycheck three weeks after July 1, about 50 days pass without pay. See [paycheck gap](/read/mechanisms/paycheck-gap).
5. **The first paychecks.** Federal withholding treats each paycheck as if it were paid all year ([IRS Pub. 15-T](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p15t)). See [annualized withholding](/read/mechanisms/annualized-withholding).
6. **The [grace period](/read/glossary/grace-period) ends, six months after graduation** ([34 CFR 685.207](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-685.207)). The options are [RAP](/read/glossary/rap), [Tiered Standard](/read/glossary/tiered-standard), IBR where the loans still qualify, or mandatory [residency forbearance](/read/glossary/residency-forbearance). RAP's first payment is priced on the last student-year return. See [student-year pricing](/read/mechanisms/student-year-pricing).
7. **Each month after grace.** On-time RAP payments at a qualifying employer count toward [PSLF](/read/glossary/pslf)'s 120; residency forbearance months do not. See [uncounted forbearance](/read/mechanisms/uncounted-forbearance).
8. **The first April.** The first resident return is due April 15 of the year after the start. It covers about half a year of pay and, at the first recertification, prices the second year of loan payments.

The [residency overview at Andwise](https://www.joinandwise.com/residency) shows the same sequence with each state's license and tax facts.

## Failure modes

Each failure below comes from a rule or a schedule, named as a mechanism.

- **The license is pending on July 1.** The board's processing time ran past the start date; in Florida, training may not begin until the registration number is issued ([Florida Board of Medicine](https://flboardofmedicine.gov/resident-physicians-interns-fellows-and-house-physicians/)). Mechanism: [license lead time](/read/mechanisms/license-lead-time).
- **The deposit and the move go on credit.** Costs arrive in June and the first paycheck in late July. Mechanism: [paycheck gap](/read/mechanisms/paycheck-gap).
- **The first return shows a refund of more than a thousand dollars.** For a single resident in Texas on a $70,000 stipend, Andwise's plan estimates about $1,318, withheld from July to December at a full-year rate. Mechanism: [annualized withholding](/read/mechanisms/annualized-withholding).
- **A $10 payment, then $76, then $344.** RAP recertifies on a student year, then a part year, then a full year. Mechanism: [student-year pricing](/read/mechanisms/student-year-pricing).
- **Three years of training with no PSLF progress.** Every residency forbearance month is uncounted, and interest accrues: about $672.50 a month per $100,000 at 8.07%. Mechanism: [uncounted forbearance](/read/mechanisms/uncounted-forbearance).

## Checklist

Each action takes 10 minutes or less and applies one mechanism.

- [ ] [Apply for the training license](/read/toolkit/apply-for-training-license): the board's apply-by date, counted back from the start date. Tool: [joinandwise.com/residency/&lt;state&gt;](https://www.joinandwise.com/residency).
- [ ] [Build the July cash buffer](/read/toolkit/build-july-cash-buffer): the days without pay and the cash they need. Tool: [Match money plan](https://www.joinandwise.com/residency/plan/texas) (replace `texas` with the state matched).
- [ ] [Check the W-4](/read/toolkit/check-the-w4): projected withholding against projected tax, and the question about the part-year method.
- [ ] [Compare RAP and residency forbearance](/read/toolkit/compare-rap-and-forbearance): payments and PSLF months against accrued interest. Tool: [repayment comparison](https://www.joinandwise.com/loans/repayment).
- [ ] [Submit the PSLF employment certification](/read/toolkit/submit-pslf-certification): the employer's status, confirmed on [studentaid.gov/pslf](https://studentaid.gov/pslf/).

## Questions this guide does not answer

Which repayment option fits a given resident, whether to change a W-4, and which program to rank are decisions for the reader. The guide gives the rules and the numbers; a loan servicer, payroll office, tax professional or the program's GME office answers for a specific case.
