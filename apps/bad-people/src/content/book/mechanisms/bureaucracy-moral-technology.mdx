title: "Bureaucracy as Moral Technology"
definition: "Procedures transform compliance into moral cover, allowing harm to be treated as acceptable if the process was followed."
is:
  - "A reliance on paperwork or policy to replace moral judgment."
  - "A system that treats procedural compliance as innocence."
isNot:
  - "A fair process that actually changes outcomes when harm appears."
  - "A single form or policy document in isolation."
predictions:
  - "People cite procedure instead of engaging with harm."
  - "Requests for exceptions are reframed as rule-breaking rather than accountability."
  - "The most visible work is documentation, not repair."
tests:
  - "Ask what happens if the process is followed and harm persists; if nothing changes, the mechanism is active."
  - "Look for phrases like 'we followed policy' used to shut down discussion of impact."
---

- Keep examples focused on procedural cover, not villainy.
