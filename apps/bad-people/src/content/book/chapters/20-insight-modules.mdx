Each module follows the insight → change → enable sequence.

## System transfer: “The system isn’t failing; it’s succeeding at transfer.”
- **Insight:** Surface how costs move downward while authority stays upward.
- **Change:** Replace blame with accountability routing.
- **Enable:** Decision owner trace tool + transfer-success examples.

## Bureaucracy as moral technology
- **Insight:** Procedure is being used as moral cover.
- **Change:** Treat policy as a decision with an owner.
- **Enable:** Paper trail leverage tool + policy-compliance example.

## Nice person trap (separate warmth from compliance)
- **Insight:** Warmth is being used as leverage.
- **Change:** Separate kindness from consent.
- **Enable:** Warmth boundary script + favor ask example.

## Policy as unowned judgment (decision-maker tracing scripts)
- **Insight:** Policy hides the decision-maker.
- **Change:** Demand ownership and escalation paths.
- **Enable:** Decision owner trace + paper trail leverage.

## Hidden labor tax (admin work as imposed workload; refusal policy)
- **Insight:** Cleanup work is treated as invisible duty.
- **Change:** Make the labor visible and assign ownership.
- **Enable:** Boundary script + documentation request.

## Moral fatigue as coerced empathy (boundary language scripts)
- **Insight:** Constant empathy demands deplete capacity.
- **Change:** Normalize limits without moral penalty.
- **Enable:** Warmth boundary script + glossary entry on compliance trap.

## Human crumple zones (reversal paths and repair mechanisms)
- **Insight:** People are used as buffers for system failure.
- **Change:** Require reversibility and repair paths.
- **Enable:** Reversibility check + reversal capacity examples.

## Delay as force (follow-up/CC/deadline tactics)
- **Insight:** Waiting is a form of pressure.
- **Change:** Reintroduce time boundaries.
- **Enable:** Deadline countermove + delay-as-force example.

## Consent fiction (tactics vs purity; minimize exposure, document harm)
- **Insight:** Apparent consent is extracted under constraint.
- **Change:** Shift to explicit terms and documentation.
- **Enable:** Boundary script + decision owner trace.

## Compliance ladder (pre-decided stop points and exit ramps)
- **Insight:** Compliance escalates in predictable steps.
- **Change:** Pre-decide exit ramps.
- **Enable:** Boundary script + buffer map.

## Safety/ethics as reputation membrane (reversibility/transparency/remedy checks)
- **Insight:** Safety language protects reputation, not repair.
- **Change:** Demand remedy and reversibility.
- **Enable:** Reversibility check + reputation membrane example.

## Performance of fairness (impact language and accommodation prompts)
- **Insight:** Fairness is performed without shifting outcomes.
- **Change:** Ask for concrete accommodations.
- **Enable:** Paper trail leverage + impact documentation.

## Abstraction advantage (template-ready impact narratives: dates, numbers, obligations)
- **Insight:** Abstract harm is easy to dismiss.
- **Change:** Specify measurable impact.
- **Enable:** Decision owner trace + documentation checklist.

## Bad people misdiagnosis (shift from villains to incentive changes)
- **Insight:** Villain stories obscure incentives.
- **Change:** Map the incentive loop.
- **Enable:** Transfer success + bureaucracy mechanisms.

## Boundaries as infrastructure (repeatable scripts and protocols)
- **Insight:** Boundaries fail without repeatable scripts.
- **Change:** Build protocols that survive low energy.
- **Enable:** Warmth boundary script + buffer map.

## Repair refusal → escalation (normalize escalation tactics)
- **Insight:** Refusal to repair is part of the mechanism.
- **Change:** Normalize escalation as repair, not aggression.
- **Enable:** Decision owner trace + reversibility check.

## Dignity bargain (clarity over submission)
- **Insight:** Dignity is traded for compliance.
- **Change:** Choose clarity over appeasement.
- **Enable:** Boundary script + glossary anchor.

## Reversal capacity divide (docs, allies, buffers)
- **Insight:** Appeals are uneven because capacity is uneven.
- **Change:** Build buffers in advance.
- **Enable:** Buffer map + reversal capacity example.

## Heal vs leverage (inner stability + outer tactics)
- **Insight:** Healing is not a substitute for leverage.
- **Change:** Pair internal stability with external action.
- **Enable:** Action primitives + resource list.

## Goodness without skill is extractable (core promise framing)
- **Insight:** Good intent becomes a resource others extract.
- **Change:** Treat skill as part of goodness.
- **Enable:** Boundary script + transfer success recognition.
