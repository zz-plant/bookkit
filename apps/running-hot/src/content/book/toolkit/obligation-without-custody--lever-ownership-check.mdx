mechanism: obligation-without-custody
name: "Lever ownership check"
trigger: "When someone is blamed for an outcome but cannot approve, prioritize, or unblock the work needed to fix it."
timeboxMinutes: 10
steps:
  - "Write the target outcome in one sentence."
  - "List the three levers that determine that outcome (approval, budget, sequence, policy exception, access)."
  - "Attach one named owner to each lever and mark whether they are currently accountable for harm if the lever fails."
  - "Issue one change request: align responsibility with at least one missing lever before the next cycle."
failureLooksLike:
  - "You end with a pep talk about ownership but no transfer of authority."
  - "Lever owners remain invisible or unnamed."
legitimateDemand:
  - "A published owner map where outcome accountability matches decision rights."
---

Use this to stop moralizing mismatch. Responsibility without levers is exposure, not accountability.
