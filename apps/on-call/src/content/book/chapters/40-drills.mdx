## Why drills

A procedure that has never been exercised is a collection of intentions. The drills in each toolkit primitive turn the runbook into something measurable.

## The drill set

- **Classify severity**: three sample alerts to sev-1/2/3 in under 10 minutes.
- **Identify rollback candidate**: each deploy owner states the candidate from memory at stand-up.
- **Capture reproduction**: turn a vague report into a one-line repro in 10 minutes.
- **Ramp escalation**: a mock sev-1 where ownership transfers without a single reminder.

## Verification

The runbook is working when an alert becomes a classified severity, a named owner, a written repro, and a verified recovery. The compliance matrix maps each control to the mechanism and drill that prove it.
