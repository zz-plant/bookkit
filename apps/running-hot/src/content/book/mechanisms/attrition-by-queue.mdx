title: "Attrition by queue"
definition: "A filtering mechanism where queue structure, resets, and handoffs quietly remove people from service pathways."
is:
  - "Multi-step waits with expiry windows, missed-call penalties, or restart requirements."
  - "Neutral-seeming throughput controls that disproportionately expel people with less scheduling flexibility."
isNot:
  - "Queue triage designed to reduce harm with callback protections and continuity ownership."
  - "Capacity incidents with transparent contingency plans and no penalty for delay."
predictions:
  - "Drop-off rates rise at every transfer point, especially after documentation refreshes."
  - "Completion favors people who can repeatedly re-enter queues during working hours."
  - "System dashboards celebrate low pending counts while unmet need remains high."
tests:
  - "Measure completion by cohort from first contact to final resolution, not just ticket closure."
  - "Flag queue restarts as harm events; frequent restarts indicate designed attrition."
---

Queues are not neutral pipes. Their structure determines who gets through and who pays with abandonment.
