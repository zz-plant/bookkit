Source: [34 CFR 685.207](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-685.207). Grad PLUS loans have no grace period but a six-month post-enrollment deferment ([34 CFR 685.204](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-685.204)).
