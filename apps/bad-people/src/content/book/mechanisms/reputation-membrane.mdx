---
slug: reputation-membrane
status: draft
title: "Reputation Membrane"
definition: "Safety or ethics language forms a protective membrane that shields reputations without creating real reversibility or repair."
is:
  - "A focus on appearances of care over measurable remedy."
  - "A system that treats transparency as optional while requiring positive messaging."
isNot:
  - "A safety practice that includes accountability and remedy."
  - "A genuine ethical review with power to reverse decisions."
predictions:
  - "Public commitments outpace internal fixes."
  - "Requests for reversibility are redirected to communications teams."
  - "Harm reports are handled as reputation risks first."
tests:
  - "Ask for a reversibility plan; if you receive talking points instead, the membrane is present."
  - "Check whether transparency increases or decreases after harm is reported."
---

Mechanism notes:
- Emphasize the gap between reputation work and repair work.
