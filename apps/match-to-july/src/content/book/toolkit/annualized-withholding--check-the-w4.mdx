Withholding annualizes each paycheck ([IRS Pub. 15-T, Worksheet 1A](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p15t)); the part-year employment method in section 6 of the same publication applies to an employee expecting no more than 245 days of work in the year, on a written request under penalties of perjury. The [Tax Withholding Estimator](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/tax-withholding-estimator) and [Form W-4](https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-w-4) are on irs.gov.

The [Match money plan](https://www.joinandwise.com/residency/plan/texas) estimates the first-year withholding, tax owed and refund for the state matched. A refund on the first return, and a change to the W-4, are both within the rules; which one fits is the reader's decision.
