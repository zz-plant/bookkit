---

## Binding rule

Set bounded time-to-repair targets and tie breach consequences to system owners.

## Enforceable standard

- Repair clocks start at harm detection, not queue acceptance.
- Clocks pause only for documented claimant-caused delays.
- Clock breaches trigger automatic escalation and interim protections.
- Review activity cannot substitute for resolution milestones.

## Minimum metrics

- p50/p90 time-to-repair by harm category.
- Clock breach rate and mean overrun duration.
- Compensation events triggered by breach.
