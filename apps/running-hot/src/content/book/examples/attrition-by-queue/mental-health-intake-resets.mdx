A patient completes intake at a regional mental-health line and is referred to a clinic. At first appointment, clinic staff require full re-intake because the original assessment system is incompatible. Missing one callback window sends the patient back to the regional queue.

After two resets, the patient drops out.

- **Queue filter:** handoff resets function as hidden exclusion criteria.
- **Measured success illusion:** each queue reports completed intakes; whole-path completion collapses.
- **Cost transfer:** patient absorbs repeated disclosure, waiting, and emotional activation.
- **Cooling redesign:** preserve queue position across handoffs, appoint continuity owner, and treat restart events as service failure.
