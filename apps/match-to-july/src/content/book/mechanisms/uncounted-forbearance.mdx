## Scope

The forbearance is mandatory: the servicer must grant it on request for a resident in an internship or residency program ([34 CFR 685.205](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-685.205); [studentaid.gov, forbearance](https://studentaid.gov/manage-loans/lower-payments/get-temporary-relief/forbearance)). It runs up to 12 months at a time and is renewable for the length of training.

**What accrues.** Interest accrues during forbearance on subsidized and unsubsidized loans alike. At the 2026–27 graduate unsubsidized rate of 8.07% ([studentaid.gov, interest rates](https://studentaid.gov/understand-aid/types/loans/interest-rates)), each $100,000 of principal accrues about $672.50 a month, or about $8,070 a year. Older loans carry the rate of the year they were disbursed.

**What does not count.** PSLF forgives the remaining Direct Loan balance after 120 qualifying monthly payments made while employed full time by a qualifying employer, such as a government organization or a 501(c)(3) organization ([20 U.S.C. 1087e(m)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/20/1087e); [34 CFR 685.219](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-685.219)). Residency forbearance months never count. On-time RAP payments made in the same months do, at the RAP amount, including $10.

For a May 2027 graduate making qualifying payments from the end of grace in November 2027, the earliest forgiveness date is November 2037. Three years of residency forbearance first moves it to November 2040.

**The employer rule.** A rule excluding employers ED finds to have a "substantial illegal purpose" (published 2025-10-31) was vacated by two federal district courts on 2026-06-30; ED appealed on 2026-08-27, and the appeals were pending as of 2026-08-31 ([CRS Legal Sidebar LSB11472](https://www.everycrsreport.com/reports/LSB11472.html)).

## Boundaries

- The mechanism is the forbearance's effect on time and interest. The payment amount that RAP would charge instead is [student-year pricing](/read/mechanisms/student-year-pricing).
- Whether a teaching hospital is a qualifying employer is a fact about that employer, confirmed through the PSLF employment certification.
