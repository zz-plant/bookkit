name: "Capture reproduction"
owner: diag-eng
reviewEveryDays: 30
trigger: "A report is vague or a diagnosis only works for the person holding it."
timeboxMinutes: 10
satisfies:
  - "DIAG-REPRO-01"
escalation:
  to: "diag-lead"
  when: "A repro line cannot be produced within the window or the trigger spans multiple services."
  withinHours: 2
drills:
  - "Take a deliberately vague bug report and turn it into a one-line repro in 10 minutes."
runbookSteps:
  - when: "The report is vague"
    action: "Turn the report into a repro line: trigger, expected, observed."
  - when: "The repro is long"
    action: "Trim it until each step is necessary; paste it into the incident thread."
  - when: "Someone new must continue"
    action: "Hand over with the repro line as step one of the handoff."
steps:
  - "Write trigger, expected, observed in one line."
  - "Run the repro against the current state."
  - "Paste the repro and the failing evidence into the thread."
failureLooksLike:
  - "An incident that 'only Dave can debug' because nothing is written down."
legitimateDemand:
  - "The next owner can reproduce the issue from the thread alone."
---

A repro is a claim, not a verdict. The fix must falsify it before it is called done.
