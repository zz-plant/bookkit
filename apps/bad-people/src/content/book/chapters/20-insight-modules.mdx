Each module follows the insight → change → enable sequence.

## Moral fatigue is a capacity drain (decision bandwidth extraction)
- **Insight:** What’s depleted is decision bandwidth under asymmetry, not goodwill.
- **Change:** Treat exhaustion as a signal of extraction, not weakness.
- **Enable:** Capacity drain checklist + decision-bandwidth audit prompt.

## Confusion as cost-allocation strategy
- **Insight:** Confusion is cheaper than refusal because it offloads accountability.
- **Change:** Shift from “understand better” to “identify who benefits.”
- **Enable:** Contradiction map (policy/portal/agent) + beneficiary prompt.

## Attrition intent (systems that shed users)
- **Insight:** Some systems optimize for shedding a percentage, not serving all.
- **Change:** Evaluate whether the process expects you to survive it.
- **Enable:** Attrition signals checklist + persistence threshold test.

## One-way reasonableness contract
- **Insight:** Reasonableness earns you responsibility, not reciprocity.
- **Change:** Deploy reasonableness strategically instead of defaulting.
- **Enable:** Reciprocity test + selective-withdrawal script.

## Shame as liability laundering
- **Insight:** Shame suppresses documentation, witnesses, and escalation.
- **Change:** Treat shame spikes as a signal to externalize.
- **Enable:** Shame-spike protocol + witness-invite template.

## “No one owns this” is an owned outcome
- **Insight:** Diffused accountability is an intentional design outcome.
- **Change:** Trace where ownership was severed.
- **Enable:** Ownership severance map + escalation routing question set.

## Translation labor as hidden coercion
- **Insight:** Being the bridge converts you into unpaid infrastructure.
- **Change:** Refuse to translate when it masks dysfunction.
- **Enable:** Translation-refusal script + friction exposure note.

## Kindness without power decays
- **Insight:** Care without leverage becomes self-betrayal.
- **Change:** Pair kindness with boundaries, documentation, and consequences.
- **Enable:** Kindness-with-power checklist + boundary reminder.

## Mis-timed action (temporal leverage)
- **Insight:** The effective window is earlier and narrower than people think.
- **Change:** Act before escalation becomes theater.
- **Enable:** Early-action trigger list + temporal leverage timeline.

## Frontline workers as upstream crumple zones
- **Insight:** Workers absorb harm to protect abstractions, just upstream.
- **Change:** Engage the role and constraints, not the person.
- **Enable:** Constraint-probe questions + role-respect script.

## “Bad people” as the edge of invisible constraints
- **Insight:** Apparent malice often reflects stripped discretion.
- **Change:** Probe constraint geometry instead of moralizing.
- **Enable:** Constraint scan worksheet + incentive loop map.

## Repair language vs complaint language
- **Insight:** Repair seeks ownership realignment; complaints seek validation.
- **Change:** Force a decision by naming the transfer and the harm.
- **Enable:** Repair demand template + ownership anchor checklist.

## Reversal capacity as recoverability
- **Insight:** Power is the ability to reverse harm after it happens.
- **Change:** Prioritize reversibility over abstract fairness.
- **Enable:** Reversibility audit + exit-ramp inventory.

## Moral fatigue spread (withdrawn surplus care)
- **Insight:** Over-extracted people conserve capacity by withdrawing care.
- **Change:** Rebuild generosity safely instead of self-shaming.
- **Enable:** Capacity rebuilding plan + surplus-care boundary guide.

## The deepest permission
- **Insight:** You are not required to absorb harm to prove goodness.
- **Change:** Be precise, escalate without apology, and stop over-explaining.
- **Enable:** Permission script + escalation checklist.
