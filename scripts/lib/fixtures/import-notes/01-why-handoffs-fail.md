# Why handoffs fail

Most incidents don't fail at the fix. They fail at the **handoff**, when context lives in one person's head.

See [the handoff checklist](./03-handoff-checklist.md), the [glossary](04-context-glossary.md#context-debt), and the [notes index](README.md).

## Context debt

Restart `{service}` only after you page <on-call-lead>. A good handoff takes <10 minutes and costs {nothing}.
