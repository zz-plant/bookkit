---
slug: compliance-trap
status: draft
title: "Compliance Trap"
definition: "Warmth and niceness are used to secure compliance, making refusal feel like cruelty or betrayal."
is:
  - "A social setup where refusal is framed as harm while compliance is framed as kindness."
  - "A situation where relational tone is used to bypass consent."
isNot:
  - "Genuine mutual care that supports refusal without penalty."
  - "Ordinary politeness without leverage attached."
predictions:
  - "Requests are made with emotional framing rather than clear terms."
  - "Refusal triggers guilt, social cooling, or accusations of being difficult."
  - "People comply while feeling resentful or depleted."
tests:
  - "Remove the warmth and restate the ask plainly; if the request weakens, the trap is present."
  - "Check whether 'no' changes the relationship status; if it does, compliance is being extracted."
---

Mechanism notes:
- Keep the mechanism about leverage, not about kindness itself.
