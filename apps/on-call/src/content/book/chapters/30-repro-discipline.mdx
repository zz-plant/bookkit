## Why the repro line

A diagnosis that only lives inside one person's head will stall at the next handoff. The written repro gives the next owner a stable reference point and gives the fix something to falsify.

Use **capture reproduction** whenever a report is vague or a diagnosis works only for its author.

## The format

Trigger → expected → observed. One line, then paste it and the failing evidence into the thread.

## Handoff rule

The next owner can reproduce the issue from the thread alone. If they cannot, the repro is not load-bearing — keep trimming until it is.
