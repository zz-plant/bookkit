## Scene

A tenant reports a serious habitability issue. The landlord logs requests as "in progress" while requiring repeated photos, new forms, and reinspection scheduling. The unit remains unsafe for weeks.

## Load map

- **Volatility input:** maintenance failure with health impact.
- **Routing path:** intake portal -> property manager -> vendor dispatch -> reinspect.
- **Human sink:** tenant absorbs exposure, missed work, and repeated documentation.
- **Failure point:** delay operates as leverage against complaint persistence.

## Transfer tally

- Repeated documentation requests occur after each handoff.
- Average days in unsafe conditions exceed stated service targets.
- Tenant withdrawal probability rises after each reset event.
- Cost burden moves to tenant through temporary accommodations and cleaning.

## Redesign standard

- Prohibit duplicate evidence requests without a deficiency reason code.
- Start a repair clock at first notice, not at vendor assignment.
- Preserve complaint priority across handoffs.
- Trigger automatic rent adjustment or penalty when repair clocks are breached.

## Why this matters

Housing systems often treat endurance as neutral waiting. In practice, endurance is a hidden payment extracted from residents.
