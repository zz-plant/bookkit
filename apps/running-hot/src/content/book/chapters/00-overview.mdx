## Purpose

- Establish the book spine: **Expose -> Formalize -> Prove -> Bind -> Reframe**.
- Distinguish state from mechanism: "running hot" names stress; externalization names design.
- Show that institutions run by moving volatility somewhere, and absent designed cooling they dissipate into people.
- Keep the governing claim explicit: **resilience without internalization is extraction**.

## Arc

### Expose (Part I)
- Make the pattern visible: obligation without custody, harm as normal operations, and people used as hidden infrastructure.

### Formalize (Part II)
- Define load paths, transfer mechanisms, and the queue/discretion machinery that routes strain.

### Prove (Part III)
- Use domain case files with one structure: **scene -> load map -> transfer tally -> redesign**.

### Bind (Part IV)
- Turn analysis into enforceable rules: cheap recourse, undo buttons, repair clocks, burden ceilings, and exit rights.

### Reframe (Part V)
- Recast justice as allocation of stability costs: who absorbs volatility, who gets reversibility, who is protected.
- Close with the indictment-level rule: do not turn people into infrastructure.
