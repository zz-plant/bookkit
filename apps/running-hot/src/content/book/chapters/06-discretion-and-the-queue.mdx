## Claim

Queue design and discretionary review can function as extraction machinery.

## Queues as moral technology

Attrition by queue turns waiting into a filter. The system does not deny you; it asks you to wait until you cannot. Multi-step waits with expiry windows remove those with less scheduling flexibility. Neutral-seeming throughput controls expel the vulnerable.

Measure completion by cohort from first contact to final resolution. Flag queue restarts as harm events. Frequent restarts indicate designed attrition, not accidental delay. When the queue resets, the burden shifts back to the person who needs help.

## Documentation as toll

Access depends on producing and re-producing documents. This is the documentation tollbooth. Eligibility is not stable; it is a repeated proof. You must demonstrate legitimacy again and again.

The cost is not just time. It is the cognitive load of personal case management. It is the financial cost of bridge spending while waiting for approval. The system extracts energy by demanding proof of need.

## Appeals as stamina test

The appeal process is not a safeguard. It is a stamina test. The reversibility gap means harm is imposed quickly but restored slowly. You must fight to return to baseline.

Sample matched cases across reviewers. Compare decision variance. If outcomes vary significantly by reviewer or shift, the gate is opaque. Require reason codes specific enough to contest. Generic codes indicate gate opacity.

## "It's complicated" as discretion procurement

Discretion as gate allows opaque judgment to determine access. Policy language delegates broad exceptions without guardrails. "It's complicated" becomes a procurement of discretion. It shifts the burden of proof entirely onto the applicant.

Case outcomes vary with weak explanation quality. Policy delegates without inspectable criteria. This is not professional judgment bounded by explicit standards. It is a decision architecture where access is arbitrary.

## When review replaces repair

Review becomes a substitute for action. The institution preserves output by routing shocks into people. The crumple zone absorbs the volatility.

The institution assigns responsibility for outcomes to people who do not control the governing levers. This is obligation without custody. You are responsible for the result, but you cannot fix the system. The queue manages the delay. Discretion manages the exception. Together, they extract compliance.
