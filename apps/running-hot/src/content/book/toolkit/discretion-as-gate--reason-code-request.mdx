mechanism: discretion-as-gate
name: "Reason-code request"
trigger: "When a decision is denied or deferred with vague language ('not eligible', 'not supported', 'case-by-case')."
timeboxMinutes: 10
steps:
  - "Request the specific decision criterion used and the evidence considered."
  - "Ask for the reason code in writing and where the criterion is documented."
  - "Request calibration path: how similar cases are reviewed for consistency."
  - "File one narrow appeal on criterion mismatch, not broad fairness rhetoric."
failureLooksLike:
  - "You receive only generic language with no contestable criterion."
  - "Appeal channel exists but cannot reference reason codes."
legitimateDemand:
  - "A contestable explanation linked to documented standards and an appeal route."
---

Discretion can stay human while still being inspectable. Reason codes are the minimum surface for accountability.
