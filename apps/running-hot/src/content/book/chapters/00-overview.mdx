## The claim

Institutions keep running by moving volatility somewhere. Where no one has built a place for it to go, it goes into people: their time, their paperwork, their vigilance, their bodies.

"Running hot" names the stress. Externalization names the design that produces it. This book is about the design, and it holds one rule throughout: **resilience without internalization is extraction**.
