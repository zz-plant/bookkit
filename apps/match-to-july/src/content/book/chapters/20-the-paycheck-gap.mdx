Two payroll facts shape July: when the first paycheck arrives, and how much federal tax it withholds. Neither is a choice the resident makes, and both are visible before the move.

## The gap

Federal loan disbursements cover the school term; the residency stipend starts with the first payday on the hospital's calendar. With a May graduation, a July 1 start and a first paycheck three weeks later, Andwise's [Match money plan](https://www.joinandwise.com/residency/plan/texas) counts 52 days from May 31 to July 22. Over those days come the move, a deposit and often first-month rent, and the [training license](/read/glossary/training-license) fee.

The plan's default inputs (replace them with real ones) come to $10,589:

| Item | Default |
| --- | --- |
| Moving | $2,500 |
| Deposit and rent paid up front | $3,200 |
| Training license and fees | $100 |
| Living costs for 52 days at $2,800 a month | $4,789 |

Some [residency agreements](/read/glossary/residency-agreement) include relocation pay or a living allowance that shortens the gap ([Andwise, 2023-02-08](https://www.joinandwise.com/blog/a-medical-students-first-contract-what-to-know-about-the-gme-residency-agreement)).

## The withholding

Payroll multiplies each paycheck by the number of pay periods in a year before looking up the tax ([IRS Pub. 15-T, Worksheet 1A](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p15t)). A resident paid from late July to December earns about 5.3 months of pay, and each paycheck is withheld as if it were twelve. For a single resident in Texas on a $70,000 stipend, Andwise's plan estimates about $2,789 withheld in 2027 against about $1,471 owed, a refund of about $1,318 on the return due April 15, 2028.

Publication 15-T also describes a part-year employment method: on a written request from an employee who expects to work no more than 245 days in the year, the employer figures withholding on the wages actually paid in the term of employment. Whether a hospital's payroll offers it is a question for payroll.

## Questions for payroll and the GME office

- When is the first payday, and which days does it cover?
- Does the program pay relocation, an advance or a housing stipend, and when?
- Does payroll use the part-year employment method on written request?
