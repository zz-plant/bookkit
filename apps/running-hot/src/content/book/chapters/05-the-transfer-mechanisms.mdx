## Channels of transfer

- Time (waiting)
- Administrative burden (documentation, repetition)
- Emotional labor (vigilance, escalation)
- Financial cost (fees, lost wages)
- Cognitive load (becoming your own manager)
- Reputational proof (worthiness tests)
- Legal displacement (litigation as substitute for repair)
