slug: obligation-without-custody

title: "Obligation without custody"
definition: "A control mismatch where institutions assign responsibility for outcomes to people who do not control the governing levers."
is:
  - "A person is judged on timeliness, correctness, or compliance while approvals, system access, and sequencing are controlled elsewhere."
  - "Failure is individualized even when the blocked lever is organizational."
isNot:
  - "A role that includes genuine decision rights, budget authority, and escalation power."
  - "A short handoff delay with clear owner and compensating controls."
predictions:
  - "Performance pressure rises at the edge while authority concentration rises at the center."
  - "Coaching language targets personal effort rather than leverage design."
  - "Escalations require social capital instead of contractually defined rights."
tests:
  - "Ask: can the responsible person approve, unblock, or reroute work without special favors?"
  - "Map one failed outcome to lever ownership; if the person blamed cannot touch the failed lever, custody is missing."
---

When responsibility and control split, institutions call it accountability while workers and users experience it as chronic exposure.
