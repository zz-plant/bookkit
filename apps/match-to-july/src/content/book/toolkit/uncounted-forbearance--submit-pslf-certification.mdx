PSLF counts 120 qualifying monthly payments made while employed full time by a qualifying employer ([20 U.S.C. 1087e(m)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/20/1087e); [34 CFR 685.219](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-685.219)). The certification records the employment; months in residency forbearance stay uncounted whatever it says ([34 CFR 685.205](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-685.205)). The Help Tool and form are at [studentaid.gov/pslf](https://studentaid.gov/pslf/).

The [Match money plan](https://www.joinandwise.com/residency/plan/texas) shows the earliest forgiveness date from the graduation month, and the [repayment comparison](https://www.joinandwise.com/loans/repayment) shows which plans count.
