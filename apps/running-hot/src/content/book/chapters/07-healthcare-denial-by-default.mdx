## Scene

A member loses medication access after an automated coverage mismatch. Prior authorization and eligibility corrections route through separate teams. Each team can delay the case, but no single team owns restoration.

## Load map

- **Volatility input:** data mismatch + high clinical urgency.
- **Routing path:** eligibility queue -> prior-auth queue -> appeals queue.
- **Human sink:** patient and caregiver perform cross-team coordination.
- **Failure point:** instant denial state with slow restoration capability.

## Transfer tally

- Pharmacy rejection events increase immediately after mismatch.
- Out-of-pocket spending rises during pending review windows.
- Clinical risk escalates while appeal status remains informational only.
- Staff effort shifts from treatment to paperwork and callback loops.

## Redesign standard

- Require provisional reinstatement when dispute evidence is verified.
- Bind each case to one continuity owner across eligibility and authorization teams.
- Publish a maximum time-to-repair threshold with automatic escalation.
- Track and compensate delay-caused costs.

## Why this matters

Healthcare systems often measure denial rate but not denial duration. Harm is dominated by duration when treatment interruption is time-sensitive.
