The rules behind each column: RAP's income measure, $10 minimum and interest waiver are in [20 U.S.C. 1087e(q)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/20/1087e) and [34 CFR 685.209](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-685.209); residency forbearance is in [34 CFR 685.205(a)(3)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-685.205). The [repayment comparison](https://www.joinandwise.com/loans/repayment) sets RAP, IBR and Tiered Standard side by side with PSLF.

This is a comparison of numbers, not a recommendation. The PSLF column assumes a qualifying employer; the [PSLF employment certification](/read/toolkit/submit-pslf-certification) confirms that.
