## Transfer success

- **Recognition:** A decision harms people who did not decide it.
- **Exoneration:** The harm is structural, not a single bad actor.
- **Mechanism:** Authority stays up while cost and volatility travel down.
- **Agency:** Use the decision owner trace tool.

## Bureaucracy as moral technology

- **Recognition:** "We followed policy" replaces moral engagement.
- **Exoneration:** People comply because procedures are enforced.
- **Mechanism:** Procedure converts compliance into moral cover.
- **Agency:** Use the paper trail leverage tool.

## Compliance trap

- **Recognition:** Warmth is used to make refusal feel cruel.
- **Exoneration:** You are not wrong for needing capacity.
- **Mechanism:** Relational tone is used as leverage.
- **Agency:** Use the warmth boundary script.

## Coercive waiting

- **Recognition:** Waiting shrinks options and raises costs, especially when there is no repair clock.
- **Exoneration:** You are reacting to time pressure, not indecision.
- **Mechanism:** Delays create coercive scarcity.
- **Agency:** Use the deadline countermove.

## Reputation membrane

- **Recognition:** Safety language rises while repair stalls.
- **Exoneration:** You are not asking for too much by demanding remedy.
- **Mechanism:** Reputation is protected without reversibility.
- **Agency:** Use the reversibility check.

## Reversal capacity

- **Recognition:** Appeals exist, but recourse cost makes them unusable for many people.
- **Exoneration:** It is capacity, not courage, that determines reversal.
- **Mechanism:** Documentation and allies decide who can reverse harm.
- **Agency:** Use the buffer map.
