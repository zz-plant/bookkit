The loan, license and tax facts in this guide come from Andwise's data files (`loan-rules.json` and `residency-states.json`, both checked 2026-10-21), which cite these sources. Links were last opened on 2026-10-26.

- 34 CFR 685.205, forbearance: [ecfr.gov](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-685.205)
- 34 CFR 685.207, obligation to repay and grace: [ecfr.gov](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-685.207)
- 34 CFR 685.208, Tiered Standard and other fixed plans: [ecfr.gov](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-685.208)
- 34 CFR 685.209, RAP and income-driven plans: [ecfr.gov](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-685.209)
- 34 CFR 685.219, PSLF: [ecfr.gov](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-685.219)
- Forbearance: [studentaid.gov](https://studentaid.gov/manage-loans/lower-payments/get-temporary-relief/forbearance)
- Interest rates, 2026–27: [studentaid.gov](https://studentaid.gov/understand-aid/types/loans/interest-rates)
- Program changes under Pub. L. 119-21: [studentaid.gov](https://studentaid.gov/announcements-events/big-updates)
- PSLF Help Tool and form: [studentaid.gov/pslf](https://studentaid.gov/pslf/)
- PSLF employer rule litigation, CRS Legal Sidebar LSB11472 (updated 2026-08-31): [everycrsreport.com](https://www.everycrsreport.com/reports/LSB11472.html)
