## Scene

A worker is deactivated after automated risk scoring. Appeal tools exist, but standards are opaque and queue resets occur when documents are uploaded in the wrong channel.

## Load map

- **Volatility input:** false-positive risk events at scale.
- **Routing path:** automation flag -> support triage -> manual review -> final determination.
- **Human sink:** workers perform unpaid evidence production while income is paused.
- **Failure point:** discretion and reversibility are weakly governed.

## Transfer tally

- Time-to-harm is near-instant; time-to-repair is highly variable.
- Appeal success correlates with persistence and writing fluency.
- Workers incur debt and opportunity loss during unresolved states.
- Reviewer variance is large for factually similar cases.

## Redesign standard

- Publish reason codes that map to contestable criteria.
- Offer provisional reactivation under monitored constraints for disputed cases.
- Audit outcome variance across reviewers and channels.
- Define continuity rules so submission mistakes do not reset queue position.

## Why this matters

Platform governance can look neutral while embedding labor extraction through procedural opacity and restoration lag.
