The [Match money plan](https://www.joinandwise.com/residency/plan/texas) computes the gap from the graduation month and an estimated first payday; with a May graduation and a first paycheck three weeks after July 1, it counts 52 days. Its inputs are assumptions until replaced with the reader's own, and they stay in the browser's address bar, not on a server.

Relocation pay and living allowances appear in some residency agreements and not others ([Andwise, 2023-02-08](https://www.joinandwise.com/blog/a-medical-students-first-contract-what-to-know-about-the-gme-residency-agreement)). The [residency overview](https://www.joinandwise.com/residency) lists the sequence the costs fall in.
