## Why triage first

An unclassified alert has no safe first action. Triage is a time budget, not a diagnosis.

Use **classify severity** when the pager fires. Use **ramp escalation** when the incident outgrows the current owner.

## The triage loop

1. Classify to sev-1/2/3 within 10 minutes.
2. Name a single owner in the thread.
3. If the window is close to firing, ramp to the named target.

## Why escalation must be bounded

A broadcast query becomes noise nobody owns. The procedure states the target, the trigger, and the window up front.

Use the drills to keep both mechanisms sharp between incidents.
