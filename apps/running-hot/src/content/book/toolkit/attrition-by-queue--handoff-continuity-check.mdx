name: "Handoff continuity check"
trigger: "When a person is told to restart from intake after waiting or submitting materials."
timeboxMinutes: 10
steps:
  - "Capture current stage, wait duration, and handoff point where reset occurred."
  - "Ask whether prior steps can be honored without restart and who authorizes that exception."
  - "Request one continuity owner across the next two stages."
  - "Escalate if restart is mandatory without reason code tied to policy."
failureLooksLike:
  - "Every handoff behaves like a new case with no continuity obligation."
  - "Drop-off is treated as user choice instead of system loss."
legitimateDemand:
  - "Continuity guarantees that preserve queue position and prior evidence across handoffs."
---

Most attrition is engineered by resets. Continuity ownership is the countermeasure.
