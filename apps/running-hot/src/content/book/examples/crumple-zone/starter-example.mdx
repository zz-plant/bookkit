A clinic launches a "digital-first" messaging portal without adding triage staff.

During flu season, message volume triples. The institution stays technically open, but response times stretch from 24 hours to 8 days. Patients are told to "be persistent" and "self-advocate" if symptoms worsen.

Nurses now spend late hours sorting urgent from non-urgent requests, writing apologetic updates, and coaching patients through insurance wording. Official dashboards still report continuity because every message is eventually touched.

Heat management is happening, but through humans:

- **Volatility / heat:** demand spike + uncertainty.
- **Engine behavior:** throughput preserved by stretching queues.
- **Heat sink:** nurses and patients absorb lag, paperwork, and emotional load.
- **Missing dissipation:** no surge staffing, no emergency vent for high-risk messages, no rollback to phone triage coverage.

The mechanism is not "people need more resilience." The mechanism is unmanaged thermal load externalized into human bodies and time.
