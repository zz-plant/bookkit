## P0 reader reflexes (non-negotiable)

- Follow the load: when harm appears, identify who is absorbing volatility.
- Distinguish delay from repair: review only counts as repair when time and ownership are bound.
- Price recourse: estimate time, money, repetition, and vigilance cost before calling a process fair.
- Spot hidden buffers: identify who is doing informal coordination and what formal mechanism is missing.
- Evaluate stability as cost allocation: ask where uptime cost is paid, not only whether uptime was preserved.
- Detect invisible extraction: test whether friction is a safeguard or strain transfer.
