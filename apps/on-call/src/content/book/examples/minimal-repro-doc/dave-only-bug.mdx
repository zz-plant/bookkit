A support ticket says "orders fail sometimes after 9pm." The senior engineer who understands it is off shift. Nobody can verify a fix because nothing reproduces on paper. A ten-minute capture step — trigger → expected → observed, pasted into the thread — would let the next owner test the diagnosis instead of trusting it.
