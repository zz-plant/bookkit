Two processes run between Match Day and the start date, on two different clocks. The state medical board issues the [training license](/read/glossary/training-license); the hospital runs credentialing and onboarding. The program's onboarding instructions usually cover both, but only the board's rule says when the license application is late.

## The board's clock

As of 2026-10-21, Andwise's state data lists 42 of 51 jurisdictions requiring a training license or permit, 5 where it depends (Alabama, Kentucky, Tennessee, Washington and Wyoming), and 4 that exempt residents in accredited programs (Arkansas, Maryland, New York and Utah). In Kentucky, for example, first-year residents are exempt and the license is required from PGY-2 ([Kentucky Board of Medical Licensure](https://kbml.ky.gov/physician/Pages/Types-of-Licenses.aspx)). In Maryland, state law lets residents practice within their program without a license ([Health Occupations 14-302](https://mgaleg.maryland.gov/mgawebsite/Laws/StatuteText?article=gho&section=14-302&enactments=false)).

Where a board publishes a date, it is one of three kinds:

| Kind | Example | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| An apply-by window in rule | Texas: 60 to 120 days before the program starts | [Texas Medical Board](https://www.tmb.texas.gov/apply-renew/physician/physician-apply/physician-in-training-permit-application) |
| A minimum lead time | DC: no later than 90 days before clinical training | [DC Health](https://dchealth.dc.gov/service/board-medicine-medical-training-license-and-registrant-application) |
| A processing time | Michigan: typically 4 to 6 weeks from receipt | [Michigan LARA](https://www.michigan.gov/lara/bureau-list/bpl/health/hp-lic-health-prof/medical/medical-doctor-licensing-guides-and-faqs/md-educational-limited-licensing-guide-and-faqs) |

For a July 1 start in Texas, the 60-day minimum falls on May 2 and the 120-day window opens on March 3. Of the 47 jurisdictions that require or may require a license, 27 publish no lead time; for those, the GME office's deadline is the working date. Each state's rule, fee and source are on its page at `joinandwise.com/residency/<state>`, for example [Texas](https://www.joinandwise.com/residency/texas).

## The employer's clock

Hospital credentialing asks for identity documents, an NPI ([NPPES](https://nppes.cms.hhs.gov/)), immunization records, life-support certifications and, for some programs, a DEA registration ([DEA Diversion Control](https://www.deadiversion.usdoj.gov/)). A physician who has been through it describes the process taking "a few weeks to several months" ([the physician's guide to credentialing](https://www.joinandwise.com/blog/the-physicians-guide-to-credentialing)).

## Questions for the GME office

- Which date governs the license application: the board's rule or the office's deadline?
- Which documents does the program send to the board, and which does the resident?
- If the license is pending on the start date, what happens to the start date and to pay?
