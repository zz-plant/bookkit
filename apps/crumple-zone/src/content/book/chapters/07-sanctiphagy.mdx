## The Extraction of Goodness

Sanctiphagy is the mining of moral behavior. It is not hypocrisy. It is not individual failure. It is structural design.

Systems need legitimacy. They do not always have time to build it. They take it from those who already have it.

A person acts with care. They show up. They stay late. They fix the leak. The system records the action. The system brands the actor as "dedicated." The system extracts the reputational value. The system discards the actor.

The virtue remains. The person does not.

This is not about the company lying. It is about the company using your integrity as fuel. Your goodness becomes a marketing asset. Your exhaustion becomes the price of that asset.

You are told you are valued. You are not. You are consumed.

## The Sanctification of Harm

Wound worship follows extraction. It silences the cost.

Suffering is celebrated. The pain is treated as sacred. The structural cause is ignored.

When you speak of the damage, you are asked to honor the wound. You are not asked to fix the machine. The wound becomes a badge of honor. It becomes proof of your virtue. It becomes a barrier to your complaint.

To demand change is to dishonor the sacrifice. To ask for repair is to admit the system failed. The system cannot admit failure. It must honor the victim instead.

The wound is weaponized. It shuts down dissent. It prevents critique. It protects the extractor.

## The Discarded Husk

The cycle completes when the virtue is gone. The person is hollowed out. The system moves on.

The language of ethics is co-opted. It justifies the extraction. It masks the void left behind.

You are asked to perform goodness. You are not asked to survive it.

The system does not value your ethics. It values the signal your ethics send to others. It values the brand. It does not value the body that produces it.

## Structural Alignment

What would it look like to stop this?

It requires refusing the extraction. It requires rejecting the sacredness of the wound. It requires demanding repair before honor.

Identify the virtues extracted from you. Name the institution that branded them. See the husk left behind.

Demand structural alignment. Not branding. Not honor. Alignment.

The cost must be absorbed by the system. Not by you.
